The camera starts a gentle orbit after 12 seconds without interaction. Turn Auto-tour off to keep a fixed view. Any drag immediately returns camera control to you.

Two clocks: the room/operating model runs in real time (with a small illustrative room thermal mass); the motion slider scales all rotating parts and tracer flow together. At 0×, the mechanical view freezes while telemetry continues. At 1×, fast blades may visually alias.

Model: generic cooling-only inverter split system. Indoor envelope 778 × 295 × 272 mm; outdoor envelope 765 × 550 × 285 mm. Internal parts, spacing between units, tube diameter, and routing are educational approximations. The rotary compressor uses an eccentric roller tangent to its chamber and a geometrically constrained sliding vane. A conventional illustrative outdoor metering valve is shown; production routing varies by model.

Experiment controls: room heat input adds 0–2 kW; active coil area scales capacity and fin height; fin conductivity changes the straight-fin efficiency η = tanh(mL)/(mL), m = √(2h/kt), with h = 35 W/(m²·K), t = 0.15 mm, L = 25 mm. Capacity scales with active area × η/η(aluminum). This is an illustrative sensitivity model, not a complete heat-exchanger solver. The room balance is C dT/dt = 0.045(Tout − Troom) + Q̇ gain − Q̇ cooling, with C = 90 kJ/K.

Thermodynamics: capacity follows compressor load and fan setting. A lift-based COP estimate drives electrical input; Q̇ out = Q̇ in + Ẇ. Pressures use an illustrative saturation-pressure approximation, not an R32 property table. No humidity, defrost, refrigerant charge, or fault model is included.

Inspired by the Teardown architecture reference. Cycle reference: PNNL / Building America. Dimensions: Daikin FTXM-R and RXM-R. External references need internet; the simulator does not.
